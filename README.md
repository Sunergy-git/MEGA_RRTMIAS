# MEGA-RRTMIAS

## Marine Engineering Gateway for Real-Time Monitoring, Intelligence, Analytics & Simulation

> A modular simulation-driven architecture for adaptive monitoring, operational analytics, and digital twin development in marine propulsion and power systems.

---

## Project Vision

MEGA-RRTMIAS explores the convergence of marine engineering, industrial automation, and operational analytics.

The project investigates how sensor data, vessel operating conditions, propulsion behavior, power management systems (PMS), and physics-based models can be integrated into a unified decision-support architecture for offshore and specialized vessels.

Rather than replacing existing onboard automation systems, MEGA-RRTMIAS aims to provide contextual understanding of vessel operation through simulation, digital twins, and engineering analytics.

---

## Primary Objectives

- Adaptive operational monitoring
- Physics-informed digital twin development
- Marine propulsion system analytics
- Hybrid power system integration
- Power Management System (PMS) analytics
- Operational efficiency assessment
- Fuel system performance evaluation
- Predictive engineering diagnostics

# Research Philosophy

MEGA-RRTMIAS is developed around the principle that engineering knowledge should guide data analytics.

The architecture combines first-principles physics, operational experience, and machine learning to improve understanding of complex marine systems.
